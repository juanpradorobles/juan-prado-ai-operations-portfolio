Operating Principles

These rules apply to every role at all times. No workflow, manager request, or homeowner demand overrides these principles.

  • All HO communication goes through Vantaca Action Items only — never personal Outlook.
  • Verify homeowner identity on every interaction before sharing account information.
  • Document everything in Vantaca. If it is not logged, it did not happen.
  • Click Update on every call, every action item, every step — never skip.
  • Always click Preview before sending any email, statement, or letter.
  • Check Activity Notes before taking action on any account.
  • Never guess balances, dates, or payment statuses — look it up or escalate.
  • Escalation is a last resort — attempt to resolve at the lowest level first.
  • Never commit payment dates, waiver approvals, or violation outcomes without authorization.
  • Written requests take precedence over verbal — encourage homeowners to submit via portal or email.
  • Collections accounts: do not discuss, do not share info — redirect to collections agency only.
  • Files uploaded to Vantaca cannot be renamed or moved after upload — name correctly before uploading.
🚫
Never send from personal Outlook. Never collect bank details by phone. Never share collections account information. These are non-negotiable across every role and every situation.

Standard Inbound Call Flow (9 Steps)
▼
  1. Greet → "Community Management Team, this is [Your Name]. May I have your account number or property address?"
  2. Open Call Center panel in Vantaca immediately — search for caller.
  3. Verify identity → "To ensure I'm reviewing the correct account, may I have your name or the account holder's name?"
  4. If caller doesn't know account info → "Could you provide the property address? I'll confirm whether your community is under our management."
  5. Identify issue type: Billing · Portal · Docs · FOB · Move · Complaint · Vendor · ARC/Work Order · Compliance · Amenity · After Hours.
  6. Resolve if within CSR scope → log → close.
  7. If cannot resolve → create Action Item → route appropriately.
  8. Log call: Action Type Homeowner Call (HO) → select Category → add notes → click Update.
  9. Close → "Thank you for calling Community Management Team. Let us know if there's anything else we can assist you with today."
⚠️
Do not transfer to Manager unless caller is aggressive/threatening or it is a genuine escalation per guidelines.

  2. Confirm account number.

▼

Escalation Decision Framework
▼

Ask all four before escalating:

  1. Has the homeowner submitted in writing (portal/email)?
  2. Is this outside CSR responsibilities?
  3. Is the caller aggressive, threatening, or mentioning legal action?
  4. Have you already attempted resolution and cannot resolve?
ℹ️
If "No" to all four → do not escalate. Encourage written submission: "We want to make sure your concern is documented correctly. The best way is to submit through your portal or email [official management email]."

Does NOT qualify for escalation:

  • Requesting docs already on portal
  • Login, balance, or general HOA questions
  • Disagreeing with standard timeframes
  • Demanding Manager without context

Emergency Protocol
▼
🚨
Life-Threatening (Fire, Crime, Medical, Gas Leak): Instruct resident to call 911 immediately. CSR does NOT coordinate, intervene, or contact emergency services.
🏠
Property Emergency (Leak, Flood, Electrical, Sewage):
1. Create urgent Service Request Action Item.
2. Mark high priority.
3. Escalate to Manager + on-call vendor per protocol.
4. Document all details clearly.

FAQ

Can tenants receive documents?
▼
No. Only the homeowner on record. Non-homeowners use HomeWiseDocs. ⚠️ HomeWiseDocs procedure not documented — see Gap G-X-03.
Portal locked out?
▼
Verify identity → Forgot Password on portal. No login → create in Vantaca: Homeowner → Login → New Login.
What qualifies as an emergency?
▼
Life-threatening (fire, crime, medical, gas leak) → direct to 911. CSR does not intervene.
Property emergency (water, flood, electrical, sewage) → urgent Service Request + escalate per protocol.
Can we email from personal Outlook?
▼
No. Vantaca Action Items only.
Who approves late fee waivers?
▼
AR for first-time waivers (per community policy). Manager for all others.
Who approves bids?
▼
Board only. AM does not approve bids.
Can we confirm violations?
▼
No. Forward to AM or Manager. CSR and AM may not confirm violations, legal positions, or fine decisions.
Document not in Vantaca?
▼
Request from ACM then send via Action Item. ⚠️ ACM contact process not defined — see Gap G-CSR-05.
Who can discuss a collections account?
▼
Collections agency only. No internal staff — not CSR, not AM, not Manager.

Never Do List

🚨
These prohibitions apply in every situation, for every role. No homeowner request, manager direction, or special circumstance overrides these rules.

All Roles — Absolute Prohibitions

  • ❌ Send any communication from personal Outlook — Vantaca Action Items only.
  • ❌ Collect bank account or payment details by phone.
  • ❌ Share information about an account in collections — redirect to collections agency.
  • ❌ Confirm violations, legal positions, or fine decisions — Manager/Legal only.
  • ❌ Send documents to unverified callers or non-homeowners.
  • ❌ Send any email, letter, or statement without previewing first.
  • ❌ Close tasks without adding notes.
  • ❌ Guess at balances — always look up or escalate.
  • ❌ Promise payment dates or waiver approvals without authorization.
  • ❌ Commit escalation resolution timelines to homeowners.
  • ❌ Rename or move Vantaca hard-coded folders (Documents > Forms > ARC / Invoice Images / Bank Statements).
  • ❌ Rename a file after it has been uploaded to Vantaca.

CSR-Specific Prohibitions

  • ❌ Transfer calls to Manager except for aggressive/threatening caller or genuine escalation.
  • ❌ Adjust account balances or ledger entries.
  • ❌ Escalate before attempting resolution.
  • ❌ Agree with homeowner complaints verbally ("you're right") without Manager confirmation.
  • ❌ Handle collections accounts beyond redirecting.
  • ❌ Issue compliance notices, violations, or hearing notices — AM/Manager only.
  • ❌ Take action on accounts without checking Activity Notes first.
  • ❌ Coordinate emergency services — direct to 911 only.

AM-Specific Prohibitions

  • ❌ Advance collection steps without Manager direction.
  • ❌ Approve bids or work orders — Manager/Board only.
  • ❌ Upload approved minutes without Manager/Board confirmation.
  • ❌ Confirm legal positions or violations to homeowners.
  • ❌ Send broadcast email without Preview and correct association confirmed.
  • ❌ Take actions in Mimic mode unless intentional — all actions are live and real on the HO account.
